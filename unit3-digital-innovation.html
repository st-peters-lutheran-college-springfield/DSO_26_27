Unit Focus

In Unit 3, you engage with and learn subject matter using the phases of the problem-solving process. You analyse end-user needs, and use problem-solving, computational, design and systems thinking to determine data, programming and user-experience requirements, before generating and prototyping a digital solution. This class uses the Intelligent Systems technology context.

  • Topic 1: Interactions between users, data and digital systems
  • Topic 2: Real-world problems and solution requirements
  • Topic 3: Innovative digital solutions
  • Technology context: Intelligent systems (ESP32-S3 + open transit data)

Internal Assessment 1: Technical Proposal

Next Bus, Made Simple — you design a non-coded, low-fidelity prototype for an ESP32-based device that presents Translink's open real-time transit data simply and accessibly for older Queenslanders with limited smartphone use. Worth 25% of Unit 3.
