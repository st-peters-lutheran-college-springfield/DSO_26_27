Week 6: Writing, Checkpoint 3 & Peer Feedback

Lessons 20–23 · Monday 9 November 2026 – Friday 13 November 2026

This Week's Lessons

Lesson 20 — Monday 9 November 2026
Week 6 · Day 1, Period 4 (60 min) · Objective(s) 8
Learning intentionCommunicate your design using written and visual features, annotations, sketches, and diagrams.
What to do
  • Write the sections explaining the interrelationships between your data structure and the passenger's experience, and your useability considerations.
  • Finalise your visual layout: place your annotated sketches, system diagram, and data samples, each with a clear caption.
  • Save a timestamped copy of your draft in your journal.

Lesson 21 — Tuesday 10 November 2026
Week 6 · Day 2, Period 2 (60 min) · Objective(s) 1–8
Learning intentionCheckpoint 3: submit your draft response for feedback.
What to do
  • Submit your draft response (script and/or storyboard for the multimodal presentation) as your teacher directs.
  • Your teacher will consult with you and sign off the checkpoint.
  • Use any remaining time to keep refining sections you know need work.
Resources
  • OneNote project journal
  • Checkpoint sign-off sheet
Add to your OneNote journalConfirmation that your Checkpoint 3 draft was submitted.

Lesson 22 — Thursday 12 November 2026
Week 6 · Day 4, Period 4 (60 min) · Objective(s) 8
Learning intentionGive and receive feedback on communication and referencing.
What to do
  • Peer feedback workshop: swap draft scripts/storyboards with a partner.
  • Using the peer feedback checklist, check your partner's referencing conventions and technical-audience language. Is every design choice explained for someone who doesn't know the solution?
  • Give feedback on clarity and conventions only. Do not copy or rewrite each other's work.
  • Record the feedback you received.
Resources
  • Peer feedback checklist (OneNote Class Notebook)
Add to your OneNote journalThe peer feedback you received.

Lesson 23 — Friday 13 November 2026
Week 6 · Day 5, Period 5 (60 min) · Objective(s) 8
Learning intentionRefine your response using feedback and finalise your referencing.
What to do
  • Redraft the weak sections identified in peer feedback (and Checkpoint 3 feedback, if returned).
  • Check your annotations total 2000 words or fewer.
  • Check your referencing against every source you cite: open data pages, existing solutions, and background reading.
Resources
  • Peer feedback (Week 6)
  • Reference list draft
Add to your OneNote journalBefore-and-after notes for your main redrafts and your current word count.
