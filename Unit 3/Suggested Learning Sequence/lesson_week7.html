Week 7: Rehearsal, Finalisation & Submission

Lessons 24–27 · Tuesday 17 November 2026 – Friday 20 November 2026

Four 60-minute lessonsLesson 27: final in-class lessonTurnitin due: Friday 27 November

This Week's Lessons

Lesson 24 — Tuesday 17 November 2026
Week 7 · Day 7, Period 3 (60 min) · Objective(s) 8
Learning intentionRehearse your multimodal presentation.
What to do
  • Rehearse the spoken and visual walkthrough of your proposal, or record a practice run.
  • Time it against the 10-minute limit.
  • Note where you rush, run over, or lose clarity, and plan your changes.
Resources
  • Draft response (near-final)

Lesson 25 — Wednesday 18 November 2026
Week 7 · Day 8, Period 5 (60 min) · Objective(s) 8
Learning intentionFinalise your annotations, visual features, referencing, and layout.
What to do
  • Incorporate your teacher's Checkpoint 3 feedback.
  • Finalise all annotations and diagram labels.
  • Proofread for grammar, referencing accuracy, and the word and time limits.
Resources
  • Teacher feedback (Checkpoint 3)
  • College style sheet
Add to your OneNote journalA list of the final changes you made from Checkpoint 3 feedback.

Lesson 26 — Thursday 19 November 2026
Week 7 · Day 9, Period 2 (60 min) · Objective(s) 1–8
Learning intentionPrepare your final submission.
What to do
  • Do a final proofread and formatting check.
  • Prepare your unified submission file(s) for Turnitin.
  • Check every requirement in the IA1 Task Overview and task sheet is covered.
Resources
  • Turnitin
Add to your OneNote journalYour final word count, running time, and completed requirements check.

Lesson 27 — Friday 20 November 2026
Week 7 · Day 10, Period 4 (60 min) · Objective(s) 1–8
Learning intentionComplete IA1 and look ahead to IA2.
What to do
  • Finalise and submit your IA1 response in class as your teacher directs. The final Turnitin submission is due Friday 27 November 2026.
  • Write a final journal entry summarising how your design developed from Week 1 to now.
  • Unit wrap-up: preview how the Intelligent Systems context carries into IA2, ‘Access & Spill Sentinel’, which extends the existing Room CH05 CO₂/temperature/humidity system with a Raspberry Pi Pico W client and an ESP32-C6 server.
Resources
  • Turnitin
  • IA2 task sheet (preview)
Add to your OneNote journalYour final summary entry and confirmation that your response is submitted.

Log Your Evidence

At the end of each lesson this week, add a timestamped entry to your OneNote project journal (see the Evidence Journal Guide). Your final entries show the finishing stages of your work and confirm your submission.
