Evidence Journal Guide

How to keep a timestamped OneNote project journal for Unit 3 IA1

Start here: set up your journal section in OneNote Class Notebook in Lesson 1, before you do anything else. It is your evidence of individual, ongoing development.

Why This Matters

IA1 is assessed against the instrument-specific marking guide. This relies on your teacher being able to see when and how you did the work, not just the finished proposal. A timestamped journal is your authentication evidence at every checkpoint.

Setting Up Your Journal

  • In your OneNote Class Notebook private section, create a page called Unit 3 IA1 Project Journal.
  • Add a new dated entry for every lesson — use Insert → Date & Time (or Alt+Shift+F) so each entry carries a real timestamp.
  • Paste or insert photos/screenshots of your work-in-progress (sketches, diagrams, notes) directly under each dated entry.

Using the Printable Evidence Log

Your teacher will also distribute an Evidence Journal template (Word document) via OneNote Class Notebook. Insert it into your journal section (Insert → File Printout) and fill in a new row every time you have evidence to record — it works alongside your dated OneNote entries, not instead of them.

Both checkpoints and the final judgement depend on your journal showing genuine progressive development. A journal that appears all at once, undated, will not count as authentication evidence.
