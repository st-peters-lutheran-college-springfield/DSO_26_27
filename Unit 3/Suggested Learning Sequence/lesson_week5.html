Week 5: Pseudocode, Checkpoints 1 & 2 & Response Structure

Lessons 16–19 · Tuesday 3 November 2026 – Friday 6 November 2026

Four 60-minute lessonsCheckpoint 1: Wednesday 4 NovemberCheckpoint 2: Thursday 5 November
Start here: two checkpoints fall this week. Have your OneNote journal open and up to date at the start of Lessons 17 and 18.

This Week's Lessons

Lesson 16 — Tuesday 3 November 2026
Week 5 · Day 7, Period 3 (60 min) · Objective(s) 4, 5
Learning intentionRefine your success criteria and symbolise your device's decision logic using pseudocode.
Direct teaching (15–20 min)Pseudocode refresher from Unit 2: assignment, sequence, selection, condition, iteration, and modularisation, using the QCAA pseudocode conventions.
What to do
  • Revisit your Week 3 success criteria alongside your sketches, system diagram, and data structure. Make sure each one is measurable and testable, and note what you changed.
  • Pseudocode workshop: write the logic that decides which state a departure is in, for example IF delay = 0 THEN … ELSE IF delay > threshold THEN …, including a branch for service alerts. Use the field names from your Lesson 12 data structure.
  • Choose your delay threshold and write one sentence justifying it for your persona.
  • Desk-check your pseudocode by hand against several rows of springfield_central_transit_snapshot.csv, including the two delayed services (routes 534 and 528).
Resources
  • Success criteria template (Week 3 draft)
  • QCAA Pseudocode Conventions Guide (Unit 2, OneNote Class Notebook)
  • springfield_central_transit_snapshot.csv
Add to your OneNote journalYour refined success criteria (with changes noted), your pseudocode, threshold justification, and desk-check results.
