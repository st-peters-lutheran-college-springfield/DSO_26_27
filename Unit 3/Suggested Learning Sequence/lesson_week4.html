Week 4: Data Structures, Sketches & System Diagrams

Lessons 12–15 · Monday 26 October 2026 – Friday 30 October 2026

Four 60-minute lessonsCheckpoints 1 & 2 next week
Start here: this is the main design week. By Friday your journal should hold everything Checkpoint 2 asks for.

This Week's Lessons

Lesson 12 — Monday 26 October 2026
Week 4 · Day 1, Period 4 (60 min) · Objective(s) 5
Learning intentionSynthesise ideas to develop the data structure your device will use.
Direct teaching (15–20 min)Revisit Resource 1: Innovation, Emerging Technologies & Intelligent Systems Components (from Lesson 9), focusing on system inputs and outputs, data streams, and internal data structures.
What to do
  • Draft the data structure your device will read from: each field's name, data type, and an example value.
  • For each field, show how it maps to the display or spoken output (for example, delay_minutes → ‘delayed’ state and the minutes shown on screen).
  • Check every field you need for the on-time / delayed / service-alert decision (Lesson 7) appears in your data structure.
Resources
  • springfield_central_transit_snapshot.csv
Add to your OneNote journalYour data structure table with field-to-output mapping.

Lesson 13 — Tuesday 27 October 2026
Week 4 · Day 2, Period 2 (60 min) · Objective(s) 1, 2
Learning intentionSymbolise ideas for the user interface using annotated sketches.
Direct teaching (15–20 min)Resource 3: Visual Communication Elements & Principles. Complete the hierarchy check for understanding before you start sketching.
What to do
  • Sketch each display state: ‘bus due in 4 min’, ‘delayed’, and ‘service alert’.
  • Annotate each sketch with at least three elements and two principles of visual communication, explaining each choice in one sentence.
  • Draft the spoken-announcement script for each state.
Resources
  • Sketching/wireframe tools: draw.io, Canva, or hand-drawn
  • Resource 3: Visual Communication Elements & Principles
Add to your OneNote journalYour annotated display-state sketches and spoken-announcement scripts.

Lesson 14 — Thursday 29 October 2026
Week 4 · Day 4, Period 4 (60 min) · Objective(s) 2
Learning intentionExplain how your data structure and the passenger's experience are connected.
What to do
  • Draw and annotate a system diagram: GTFS-RT feed → Wi-Fi-connected ESP32-S3 → 1.54″ display and speaker, with the microphone as a voice trigger.
  • Label each connection with the data that moves along it, using the field names from your data structure.
  • Add annotations explaining how specific fields become something the passenger sees or hears.
Resources
  • System diagram template (OneNote Class Notebook)
Add to your OneNote journalYour annotated system diagram.

Lesson 15 — Friday 30 October 2026
Week 4 · Day 5, Period 5 (60 min) · Objective(s) 1, 2
Learning intentionRefine your sketches and diagram, and explain the useability of your design.
Direct teaching (15–20 min)Revisit Resource 2: Useability Principles & Heuristic Evaluation. This time apply the five principles to your own design rather than an existing solution.
What to do
  • Self-review your system diagram and display-state sketches against the Comprehending criteria in the IA1 marking guide, then refine them.
  • Write annotations explaining how your design meets each useability principle (accessibility, effectiveness, safety, utility, and learnability) for your persona.
  • Keep the earlier versions in your journal so your refinements are visible.
Resources
  • IA1 Instrument-Specific Marking Guide
  • Persona (Week 2)
  • Resource 2: Useability Principles & Heuristic Evaluation
Add to your OneNote journalBefore-and-after versions of your sketches and diagram, plus your useability annotations.
