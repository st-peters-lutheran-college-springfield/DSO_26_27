IA1 Task Overview: Next Bus, Made Simple

Internal Assessment 1 · Technical proposal · 25% of Unit 3 · Issued 17 September 2026, due 27 November 2026

Start here: this page is a plain-language walkthrough. The full task sheet is the source document for everything you submit — download it below and read it properly in Lesson 1.

The Context

The Queensland Open Data and Digital Innovation Symposium invites Queensland high school students to pitch a technical proposal for an intelligent system that uses open government data to solve a real-world problem. This year's theme is ageing well and staying connected. You will explore the personal, social and economic needs of older Queenslanders who use public transport but have limited smartphone use.

Your proposal will be judged by a panel of Translink service planners, aged-care providers and software developers — professionals who won't already be familiar with your specific solution, so your proposal must clearly explain and justify your design choices.

The Task

You will apply the problem-solving process to develop a technical proposal for the symposium, using the Unit 3 technology context of Intelligent Systems. You will analyse Translink's live open transit data and design an ESP32-based device with a small display and speaker, using the kit provided, that presents this data simply and accessibly.

Possible focus areas include reducing anxiety for elderly passengers waiting at a stop with no real-time information, supporting family members or carers, or providing an audio/large-text alternative to smartphone apps.

Checkpoints

  • Checkpoint 1: identified real-world problem, analysis of the selected GTFS-RT feed, and exploration of existing solutions.
  • Checkpoint 2: development evidence — draft persona/user story, success criteria, a draft system diagram, and initial sketches.
  • Checkpoint 3: a draft response (script and/or storyboard) for feedback.
  • Checkpoint 4: final response.
